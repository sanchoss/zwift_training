## Riding with Zwift Coach

In Zwift Companion, add a route to your Plan from **Explore → Routes → Zwift Video → route → + → Add**. To ride a Coach workout on the route, open the workout in Zwift, select **Start Ride**, toggle **Zwift Video**, choose the route, and select **Start Workout**. If the workout ends before the route does, continue riding until the route-completion badge appears. For an all-out route attempt, ride the route without ERG mode and let Coach evaluate the completed activity; do not treat every day as a maximum-effort day.

Source: [Using Zwift Video](https://support.zwift.com/en_us/using-zwift-video-BJC5uClTfe), [Using Zwift Coach](https://support.zwift.com/en_us/using-zwift-coach-H1dGtRxTGe).
