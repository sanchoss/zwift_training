# Twin Lights Ride (TLR) — Sun 27 Sep 2026

> Back-to-back weekend with [Escape New York (26 Sep)](2026-09-26_Escape_New_York.md) — ridden the day before. See that file for the shared taper plan and the recommendation to stay overnight near Highlands, NJ instead of driving back from Manhattan.

## Event

- **Organizer:** Bike New York — https://www.bike.nyc/events/twin-lights-ride/
- **Route:** 100 mi — RideWithGPS: https://ridewithgps.com/routes/52538630 ("2025 TLR - 100")
- **Start:** Huddy Park, Highlands, NJ 07732
- **Start time:** 7:00 AM (arrive 45 min early for check-in/breakfast, from 6:00 AM)
- **Distance:** 160.8 km (100 mi)
- **Elevation:** 774 m gain (route GPS data — organizer page says ~3,000 ft/915m, but the actual tracked route is flatter, ~2,540 ft)
- **Terrain:** 100% paved, loop through Highlands → Oceanport → Neptune → Wall → Freehold, classified "flat" by RideWithGPS
- **Difficulty:** Hard (organizer rating) — driven by distance/coastal wind exposure more than climbing
- **Aid stations:** 6 on route, ~every 27 km — good anchor points for fueling

## Travel from home (740 W 187th St, NY 10033)

- **Distance to Huddy Park:** ~50 mi (~80 km)
- **Drive time:** ~1h15–1h30 (Highlands↔Times Square alone is 49.7 mi / 1h06m per Google-sourced estimate; Washington Heights adds a bit more distance and Sunday-morning traffic is light) — confirm with live traffic 1–2 days out
- **If driving straight from home Sunday morning:** wake ~4:00 AM, leave ~4:45–5:00 AM to arrive 6:00–6:15 AM. Doing this the morning after a 125mi/2150m day is rough.
- **Recommended instead:** stay overnight near Highlands, NJ after Saturday's Escape New York finish (see that event's file) — cuts Sunday's travel to a short local drive and buys real sleep.

## Pacing & fueling — day 2 of back-to-back

- Legs will already be fatigued from Saturday — **ride steady, no attempts at a fast time**, use all 6 aid stations rather than skipping any
- Carbs: still 60–80 g/h (slightly less climbing than day 1, but glycogen stores are already depleted from Saturday, so don't cut fueling)
- Sodium: same or higher than day 1 — cumulative dehydration risk across two days
- Extra electrolytes at wake-up and pre-start, on top of on-bike intake

## After finish

- Full recovery mode — no additional training that day
- Plan the following week (28 Sep onward) as a recovery week: easy Zwift only, no hard/VO2max days, until TSB recovers from the two-day load
