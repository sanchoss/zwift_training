## Fueling (scaled up from Nutrition_Plan.md — this is much longer than typical 1.5–2.5h rides)

- **Target 70–90 g carbs/hour**, not the usual 40–60 — use aid stations + on-bike bottles (Skratch + honey combo A/B)

## Combined weekend logistics note

Sakura Park → Huddy Park, Highlands NJ (day 2 start) is ~50 mi / ~1h15–1h30 by car. Driving that Sunday morning after a 126mi/2300m day, on top of a ~4 AM wake-up, is a bad combination. **Recommend driving to the Highlands, NJ area Saturday evening right after the ENY finish and staying overnight there**, rather than going home to Washington Heights first. This turns Sunday's morning into a short local drive instead of a 1.5h drive following almost no sleep.
