## How to Earn 10,000,000 Drops Fast

Zwift doesn't publish an exact Drops formula, but it scales with **distance covered per hour**, not raw effort — so the fastest path is maximizing average speed, not maximizing watts:

1. **Draft in a fast group instead of riding solo.** A bunch or high-end RoboPacer group holds a much higher speed than solo effort at the same power (the ~3% draft plus not having to punch through wind yourself). More km/h → more Drops/h for the same or less effort.
2. **Pick flat/fast routes over climbs.** Climbing routes have much lower average speed (15-20 km/h) than flat ones (30-40+ km/h), so even though effort feels harder, flats bank Drops faster per hour. From your own PLAN.md RoboPacer table: routes like Neon Flats / Tempus Fugit / Wandering Flats are your highest-speed options.
3. **Watch for Zwift's "Double Drops" promo windows.** Zwift periodically runs limited-time 2x Drops events (check Zwift News / Companion app) — timing a big grind session to one of these effectively halves the time needed.
4. **Minimize non-riding time.** Drops only accrue while moving — back-to-back rides with minimal menu/loading time between them beat one long ride with breaks.
5. **Check your own actual rate.** Zwift Companion shows Drops earned per completed ride. Pull your last few rides' Drops totals and divide by ride time to get your real Drops/hour at your current routes/groups — that gives an accurate ETA to 10M better than any generic estimate.
