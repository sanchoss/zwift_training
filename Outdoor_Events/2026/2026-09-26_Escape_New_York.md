## Event

- **Organizer:** New York Cycle Club — https://eny.nycc.org/
- **Route:** Randonneur's Special, 125 mi — RideWithGPS: https://ridewithgps.com/routes/48456935 ("2024 ENY (125) - Final")
- **Start:** Sakura Park, Manhattan (~122nd St / Riverside Dr)
- **Check-in:** 6:00 AM, rolling start (no fixed gun time) — festival closes 6:00 PM
- **Distance:** 201.2 km (125 mi)
- **Elevation:** ~2,149 m (7,050 ft) gain
- **Terrain:** Paved road, crosses GW Bridge into Bergen/Rockland County NJ+NY, includes Harriman State Park
- **Difficulty:** Expert (organizer rating)

## Travel from home (740 W 187th St, NY 10033)

- **Distance to Sakura Park:** ~3.2 mi (~5 km)
- **Mode:** Bike over as warm-up (~15–20 min easy spin) — no car needed, start is close enough to ride to directly
- Leave home ~5:35–5:45 AM to check in by 6:00 and roll out 6:15–6:30

## Fueling (scaled up from Nutrition_Plan.md — this is much longer than typical 1.5–2.5h rides)

- **Target 70–90 g carbs/hour**, not the usual 40–60 — use aid stations + on-bike bottles (Skratch + honey combo A/B)
- **Sodium 500–1000 mg/h**, more if hot
- Alternate solid food (dates, gels) with liquid carbs to avoid GI fatigue over 9+ hours

## After finish (critical — day 2 starts early)

1. Immediate: EAA + carbs within 30 min
2. Full dinner: carbs + protein
3. Cold shower / compression socks, light stretch
4. **Sleep by 21:00–22:00** — day 2 wake-up is brutal otherwise

## Combined weekend logistics note

Sakura Park → Huddy Park, Highlands NJ (day 2 start) is ~50 mi / ~1h15–1h30 by car. Driving that Sunday morning after a 125mi/2150m day, on top of a ~4 AM wake-up, is a bad combination. **Recommend driving to the Highlands, NJ area Saturday evening right after the ENY finish and staying overnight there**, rather than going home to Washington Heights first. This turns Sunday's morning into a short local drive instead of a 1.5h drive following almost no sleep.
